Album links give the best tags. A YouTube Music, Spotify or Apple Music album resolves the whole release in one lookup, so every track shares the real album name, year, cover and track number. Spotify and Apple Music links are read for their tracklist only — their audio is never touched.

320 kbps costs 2.4 MB per minute, so a cap and “always 320” cannot both hold. Each track gets the highest legal rate that fits: at the default 10 MB nearly every song up to 4 minutes stays at 320 kbps. Tighter caps step down the ladder (256 → 224 → 192 → 160 → 128) until the file fits.
